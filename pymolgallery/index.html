The idea
The molecule is the artwork.
The script is the recipe.

PyMOL Art Gallery is a visual inspiration gallery and styling cookbook. The browser previews are illustrative 3D views; the copied scripts are the actual PyMOL commands. Presets use common selections where practical, and comments point out the parts you may want to adapt.

Open collection / Submit a style

Have a look
worth sharing?

Send a PyMOL visualization recipe to the gallery. Keep it focused: a useful style, a short explanation, and the commands someone else can copy and adapt.

Submissions are reviewed before they become part of the public collection. No file upload is required.
The form is delivered through Web3Forms.

PyMOL Art Gallery grew from an interest in the space between pharmacy, structural biology, computational drug discovery, and scientific software — where the way a result is visualized can shape how easily it is understood.

My work spans molecular docking, molecular dynamics, DFT, drug repurposing, and small tools built for pharmacists and researchers.
