Formulation Note

The Weibull model fits well for immediate-release ibuprofen tablets. The Q45 of 82.4% exceeds the USP Stage 1 acceptance value of Q=75%, indicating adequate dissolution. Disintegration at 4:12 is well within limits.

With 8% disintegrant (sodium starch glycolate typical), consider reducing to 5% to avoid over-wetting. Current lubricant level is acceptable for tablet ejection without excessive hydrophobicity effects on dissolution.
