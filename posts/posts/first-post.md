## Why keep research notes?

Computational drug discovery sits at the intersection of chemistry, biology, statistics, and software. A small, well-organized note can preserve a useful command, a surprising result, or a question worth revisiting.

### A simple workflow

1. Record the question before running an analysis.
2. Keep the input files and important parameters reproducible.
3. Separate observed results from interpretation.
4. Link to primary sources whenever possible.

> A computational result is a hypothesis-generating result—not, by itself, proof of clinical efficacy.
